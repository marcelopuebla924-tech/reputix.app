### Supabase Security Advisor
- ERROR: `owner_business_dashboard` and `owner_business_hourly_patterns` are SECURITY DEFINER views. Their current predicates explicitly require verified ownership, approved business status, and active paid subscription, and their ACLs showed authenticated SELECT but not anon. Still needs a carefully tested `security_invoker=true` migration.
- WARN: `submit_business_proposal` is a public SECURITY DEFINER RPC. It has an empty search_path, qualified object references, input bounds, duplicate detection, and inserts pending records; the client-side honeypot can be bypassed by direct RPC calls. Server-side anti-abuse is needed.
