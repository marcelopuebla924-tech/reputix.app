## New product decision
Build a polished, dedicated private workspace for paying customers. A customer with an active paid subscription should receive the complete set of features included in their purchased plan, presented in a well-organized, professional dashboard that works on mobile and desktop and supports Spanish and English.

Keep three distinct experiences:
1. General customer workspace: account overview, profile/settings, the customer's own reviews/activity, their conversations and complaints, discovery/saved businesses when implemented, subscription status and plan actions.
2. Business-owner workspace: owner tools and analytics only for verified owners/authorized representatives. Paid status alone must never grant ownership or access to a business's private data.
3. Admin workspace: moderation, owner-claim review, platform operations and ad administration only for accounts with verified server-side admin authorization. A paid plan never grants admin privileges.

## Subscription entitlements
- Free: basic profile, reviews/ratings and customer replies as defined by the current plan catalog.
- Basic: $13 USD/month; includes the Basic plan features currently cataloged, such as featured profile, monthly statistics, review trends and business promotions.
- Pro: $25 USD/month; includes Basic plus advanced statistics, time comparisons, Reputix AI and performance history as defined by the catalog.
- Do not promise a feature in the UI until it is actually implemented and tested.
- Enforce paid entitlements on the server; hiding/showing buttons in the browser is not a security boundary.
- Keep subscription/payment state authoritative from verified backend/webhook records.
